# Sample Policy Report: Bengaluru Urban Water Resilience

## Executive summary
Bengaluru Urban is flagged as a critical water-scarcity district with high groundwater extraction pressure, low recharge, lake degradation, and urban expansion. BharatSustain AI recommends managed aquifer recharge, lake restoration, ward-scale reuse, and smart metering as the first investment bundle.

## Recommended execution roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | Actions | KPIs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | 0-90 days | mission cell, hydrogeology baseline, lake encroachment audit | verified aquifer map, priority wards |
| Phase 2 | 3-18 months | recharge wells, lake rejuvenation, wastewater reuse pilots | MLD reused, recharge capacity |
| Phase 3 | 18-60 months | scale to high-risk wards, outcome-linked financing | groundwater level recovery, tanker dependency reduction |

## Climate ROI
Estimated benefits per rupee include avoided tanker cost, reduced heat exposure through blue-green restoration, lower energy use from pumping, and improved drought preparedness.
